## Final Answer

**Liaquat Ali Khan** was the first Prime Minister of Pakistan, serving from August 14, 1947, until his assassination on October 16, 1951.

**Key Facts:**

**Role and Appointment:**
- Appointed as Prime Minister on Pakistan's independence day (August 14, 1947)
- Served under Governor-General Muhammad Ali Jinnah
- Led the government during Pakistan's critical early years

**Background:**
- Born October 1, 1895, in Karnal (now in India)
- Educated at Aligarh Muslim University and Oxford University
- Close associate of Muhammad Ali Jinnah in the Pakistan Movement
- Served as Finance Minister in the interim government before partition

**Major Achievements:**
- Established Pakistan's administrative and governmental framework
- Managed the massive refugee crisis following partition
- Introduced the Objectives Resolution (1949) - foundational document for Pakistan's constitution
- Strengthened Pakistan's foreign relations and joined the Commonwealth

**Legacy:**
- Honored with the title **"Quaid-e-Millat"** (Leader of the Nation)
- Assassinated by an Afghan assassin in Rawalpindi on October 16, 1951
- Considered one of Pakistan's founding fathers alongside Jinnah
- His wife, Begum Ra'ana Liaquat Ali Khan, was also a prominent political figure

Liaquat Ali Khan's leadership during Pakistan's formative years was crucial in establishing the nation's political and administrative foundations.
